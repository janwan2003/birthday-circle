Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Birthday Circle is a shared birthday list for groups. It is built to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no email addresses, no phone numbers, no contact imports, no ads and no tracking.

What we store

  • The circle: its name and when it was created and last opened.
  • Each birthday: the name someone typed, the day and month, and the birth year only if they chose to give it, plus whether their age may be shown.
  • A device key: your browser makes a random key for each circle and keeps it in its own storage. We store only a one-way hash of it, which lets you edit the entries you added. It does not identify you.
  • Abuse limits: a one-way hash of your IP address with a counter, kept for at most one hour, so one visitor cannot flood the service.

Who can see it

Anyone who has a circle's link can see its names and birthdays, and can subscribe to its calendar feed. Links contain a long random code, circles are not listed anywhere, and search engines are asked not to index them. Share the link only with the group it is meant for. A birth year is shown to others only if its owner ticks “Show my age”.

We never sell, rent or share the data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Calendar subscriptions

If you subscribe to a circle in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar, that calendar service downloads the circle's birthdays from us every few hours. Their own privacy policies cover what they do with your calendar.

Deleting

  • You can edit or remove any entry you added, at any time, with its Edit button.
  • The person who made a circle can remove any entry or delete the whole circle.
  • Circles that nobody opens or syncs for more than 400 days are deleted automatically.

Where it runs

The site and its database run on Cloudflare (Pages, Functions and D1). Cloudflare processes requests on our behalf and keeps standard security logs.

Support and contact

Questions, a problem, or a deletion request you can't do yourself: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/birthday-circle/issues. Please don't post a circle's link publicly; describe the circle instead.
