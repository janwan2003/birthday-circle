    Questions

    Does everyone need an account or the app?

    No. There is no app and no sign-up. The link is the circle: open it, type a first name and a date. Your phone remembers that the entry is yours so you can change it later.

    How do the reminders work?

    Tap “Add to my calendar” in the circle and choose Google, Apple (iPhone, Mac) or Outlook. Your calendar subscribes to the circle, so every birthday shows up as a yearly all-day event, and people who join later appear on their own. Google Calendar updates subscribed calendars every few hours; Apple and Outlook are similar.

    Who can see the birthdays?

    Only people who have the link. Links are long and random, circles are not listed anywhere and search engines are told not to index them. Your birth year is optional; if you give it, others only see your age if you tick “Show my age”.

    Can I add someone who isn't in the chat?

    Yes. Use “Add someone else” to add a child, a partner or a grandparent. You can edit or remove the people you added, and the person who made the circle can tidy up the whole list.

    What about 29 February?

    Leap-day birthdays show on 28 February in other years, in the list and in your calendar.

    Is it really free?

    Yes, with no ads and no tracking. We don't sell or share birthdays, and we never will. See the privacy policy.
