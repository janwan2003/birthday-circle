Birthday Circle

A shared birthday list for your group chat

Make one link for your family, friends or team. Everyone adds their own birthday in 10 seconds, and everyone gets the whole list plus reminders in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar. No app, no sign-up, free.

Open Birthday Circle

How it works

  1. Make a circle for your family, friends or team and add your own birthday.
  2. Post the link in the group chat. Everyone opens it and types their own name and birthday.
  3. Everyone sees who's next and can subscribe once in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar. People who join later appear on their own.

Privacy

No accounts, no email addresses, no tracking, no ads. Birth year is optional and hidden unless you choose to show your age. Read the full privacy policy.

Support

Questions or problems: open an issue. Please don't post a circle's link publicly.
